OUR TRAINING STANDARD

Recruitment is only the beginning.

Security personnel need more than a uniform. They need verification, training, clear responsibilities, site-specific preparation and continued supervision.

Our process combines screening, classroom and practical sessions, physical readiness, emergency preparation, SOP orientation and post-deployment monitoring.

02 Verified Records ID-verified documentation and personnel database
03 Continuous Improvement Inspection, reporting, feedback and refresher support
